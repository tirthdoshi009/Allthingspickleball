# Purpose at Pickleball

When I go to pickleball, I meet friends, but I do not always have a clear idea
of why I am there. I feel shy, and I find myself wondering what I can give
rather than only showing up to play.

My purpose can be simple: help someone feel welcome and have a good game.

I do not need to be the best player, coach people, or become unusually outgoing
to do that. A small contribution is still a contribution.

## Small Ways to Give

- Notice someone waiting alone and ask, "Do you want to join us?"
- Be a dependable partner: share the court and encourage them after a mistake.
- Ask whether a newcomer wants help with the rotation or score before explaining.
- Learn someone's name and greet them the next time.
- Do not give unsolicited advice. Helping does not have to mean teaching.

I do not need to sustain a long conversation for these actions to matter.

## One Intention for a Session

Today, I will make it easier for one person to belong here.

That is an intention, not a quota. I can notice what someone actually needs
rather than arrive with a job I must perform.

## I Can Receive, Too

Playing, enjoying my friends, and letting people get to know me are also valid
reasons to be there. I do not need to make myself useful to deserve a place on
the court.

Purpose can guide how I participate without becoming another performance
requirement.
